📜 1.0.1 版本更新日志
本次更新为模组首个公开测试版本，新增三大核心附魔，完善了“云来宗门”系列战斗体系。

🆕 新增附魔 | New Enchantments
一、云来弓法 | YunLai Archery
“云来弓术的基础心法，提升拉弓蓄力速度。”

适用装备：弓

最高等级：X（10级）

效果：提升弓的拉弓蓄力速度，等级越高拉弓越快

获取方式：附魔台可获得

兼容性：可与“力量”附魔共存

“The fundamental technique of YunLai archery, increasing draw speed.”

Applies to: Bow

Max Level: X (10)

Effect: Increases bow draw speed, higher levels draw faster

Availability: Enchanting Table

Compatibility: Compatible with Power enchantment

二、古·云来弓法 | Ancient YunLai Archery
“传承自古老云来宗门的至高弓术，拉弓与箭矢皆如流风。”

适用装备：弓

最高等级：X（10级）

效果：同时提升拉弓蓄力速度与箭矢飞行速度，满级蓄力约 0.51 秒，箭矢速度约为原版 1.95 倍

获取方式：附魔台可获得（稀有）

兼容性：可与“力量”附魔共存

“The supreme archery technique passed down from the ancient YunLai school, both draw and arrow flow like wind.”

Applies to: Bow

Max Level: X (10)

Effect: Increases both draw speed and arrow velocity; at max level, draw time ~0.51s, arrow speed ~1.95x vanilla

Availability: Enchanting Table (Rare)

Compatibility: Compatible with Power enchantment

三、风踏涟漪 | Wind Ripple
“陆上疾行，水面如履平地。”

适用装备：靴子

最高等级：I（1级）

效果：

陆地上移动速度提升至 1.5 倍

水面上可正常行走，移动速度提升至 1.25 倍

潜行时效果暂时失效

获取方式：附魔台可获得

冲突：与深海探索者、冰霜行者、灵魂疾行、迅捷潜行互斥

“Swift on land, walk on water as if it were solid ground.”

Applies to: Boots

Max Level: I (1)

Effect:

+50% movement speed on land

Walk on water, +25% movement speed on water surface

Sneaking temporarily disables the effect

Availability: Enchanting Table

Conflicts: Depth Strider, Frost Walker, Soul Speed, Swift Sneak
